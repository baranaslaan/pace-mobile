## 🇬🇧 English

### App name (≤30)
`Pace — Budget & Spending` *(25)*

### Subtitle (≤30)
`Simple, private, offline budget` *(30)*

### Promotional text (≤170)
> Add an expense in a second, see your whole month at a glance. No account, no
> ads, no tracking — your data stays on your phone.

### Keywords (≤100, comma-separated)
`budget,expense,spending,money,saving,subscription,fixed cost,finance,limit,currency,offline,privacy`

### Description (≤4000)
```
Pace is the simplest way to track your spending — no messy spreadsheets. Open
it, type the amount, done. See how your month is going on a single screen.

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account, no sign-up, no email.
• All your data stays on your phone — nothing is sent to a server.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

EVERYDAY USE
• Add an expense in seconds.
• Save frequent expenses with one tap using quick-add templates.
• Set a monthly limit and always see what's left.
• Enter your fixed costs and subscriptions once; they're counted automatically
  at the start of each month.

MULTI-CURRENCY
• Supports TRY, USD, EUR, and GBP.
• Live rates come from European Central Bank data; works offline with built-in
  fallback rates.

PACE PRO
For those who want to go deeper:
• Full analytics — forecast, trends, category breakdown, and past months.
• Unlimited fixed costs.
• Scheduled recurring expenses.
• CSV export.

Built to stay simple. Focus on the numbers you actually have.
```
